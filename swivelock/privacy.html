Swivelock: Arrow Escape – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Swivelock: Arrow Escape ("the app"), published by onrcvndev ("we"), handles information. You do not have to sign up: the app creates an anonymous player account automatically. We do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details in the app.

Your player account and game data

  • Anonymous account: on first launch the app creates an anonymous account (Firebase Authentication) with a random user ID and an automatically assigned nickname, which you can change in your profile.
  • Cloud save: your game progress (level, checkpoint, coins, lives and the best stars earned on each level) is stored on your device and also saved to Google Cloud Firestore under that account, so it can be restored.
  • Leaderboards: your nickname, best level, total stars and stars earned this week are visible to other players. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as your nickname.
  • Google Play Games (optional): if you choose "Link Google Play", your account is linked to your Google Play Games profile so you can restore your progress on another device. We then receive your Play Games player identifier through Firebase Authentication; we do not receive your email address or contacts.
  • Settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device.

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect information automatically:

These services process data according to Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Firebase privacy information.

Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time with the shield button on the app's home screen. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above for the purposes described. Your account, nickname, cloud save and leaderboard entry are kept until you delete your account. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase's default retention settings.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your profile (top left of the home screen) and tap Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, nickname, leaderboard entry and cloud save, and resets the progress on your device. You can also email onrcvndev@gmail.com with your nickname and ask us to delete your account and data; we will do so within 30 days. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your account and expire under Firebase's retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. You can change your nickname and delete your account in the app at any time; for other requests, contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Oyuncu hesabınız ve oyun verileriniz

  • Anonim hesap: uygulama ilk açılışta rastgele bir kullanıcı kimliği ve otomatik atanan, profilinizden değiştirebileceğiniz bir takma adla anonim bir hesap (Firebase Authentication) oluşturur.

  • Google Play Games (isteğe bağlı): "Google Play ile bağla"yı seçerseniz hesabınız Google Play Games profilinize bağlanır; böylece ilerlemenizi başka bir cihazda geri yükleyebilirsiniz. Bu durumda Firebase Authentication üzerinden Play Games oyuncu kimliğinizi alırız; e-posta adresinizi veya kişilerinizi almayız.
  • Ayarlar (ses, titreşim, dil) yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır.

Analitik ve çökme verileri hesabınıza bağlı değildir ve Firebase'in saklama sürelerine göre silinir.
