Swivelock: Arrow Escape – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Swivelock: Arrow Escape ("the app"), published by onrcvndev ("we"), handles information. We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details in the app.

Information stored on your device

Game progress (level, lives, coins) and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect information automatically:

ServicePurposeData collected
Google AdMobShowing ads (banner, interstitial, rewarded)Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions
Google Analytics for FirebaseUnderstanding how the game is played (e.g. level started/completed) to improve difficulty and featuresApp instance ID, device information, in-game events, approximate location derived from IP
Firebase CrashlyticsDetecting and fixing crashesCrash logs, device and operating system information, app state at the time of the crash

These services process data according to Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Firebase privacy information.

Ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent management platform. You can change your choice at any time with the shield button on the app's home screen. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above for the purposes described. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Firebase's default retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold data that identifies you directly, most requests are best handled through the controls above; you can also contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
